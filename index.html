Wheat Field with Cypresses

Vincent van Gogh (1889)

Creative Commentary

Painted from his asylum window in Saint-Rémy, Van Gogh saw this wind-swept landscape as a symbol of life's eternal cycles. His heavy, spiraling brushwork makes the wheat feel like a golden sea and the cypresses like dark green flames reaching for the sky.
