# 🏛️ MET Museum Pomodoro Timer

A beautiful, minimal Pomodoro timer integrated with the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection. Designed to bring art, mindfulness, and productivity together.

👉 **[Try the Live Timer](https://therealsrii.github.io/pomordorotimer/)**

---

## 🎨 The Concept

I made this to merge structured focus with classical art. As your focus session ticks down, a classical masterpiece is progressively revealed in full resolution on your screen. Once the timer completes, the painting is fully uncovered, inviting you to write down your reflections and curate your own personal focus gallery.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Masterpiece Reveal:** Watch high-resolution classical paintings (Van Gogh, Monet, Rembrandt, Vermeer, and more) fade into view as you work.
* **Curator Insights:** Hover over the bottom-right details capsule to instantly reveal poetic commentaries, historical context, and critiques for each piece.
* **Post-Session Reflections:** Write down your thoughts, feelings, or interpretations of the painting to log them in your personal History gallery.
* **Movable Liquid Glass UI:** A premium, semi-transparent frosted interface that can be dragged and snapped to any corner of the screen so it never blocks the artwork.
* **Sound & System Alerts:** Gentle synthesized chime tones and system-level desktop notifications alert you when sessions start and finish, even in background tabs.
* **Zero Latency Curation:** Art details, images, and commentaries are managed dynamically to ensure instant page loads and seamless transitions on startup.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 (Modern HSL variables & Backdrop Filters), JavaScript (ES6)
* **API Integration:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access API
* **Audio Synthesis:** Web Audio API (Synthesizing custom double-tone alert chimes)
* **Desktop Notifications:** Web Notifications API
